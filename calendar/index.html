🔴 Red means size down

High-impact releases like CPI, jobs and FOMC can move NQ 100+ points in seconds. Know the time before you take a trade.

⏱️ 5:30am PT is the big one

Most major US data drops at 8:30am ET (5:30am PT), an hour before the open. That move often sets the tone for the day.

📈 Forecast vs. actual

Price reacts to the surprise, not the number. A big miss or beat versus the forecast is what moves the market.
