How to read this

Green bars are prices where dealers hedge against the move: they sell rallies and buy dips. Price tends to slow down, chop or pin there.

Red bars are prices where dealers hedge with the move. Moves tend to speed up through them, up or down. Red is about speed, not direction.

Call Wall is the biggest green level above price: often the ceiling. Put Wall is the biggest put level below: often the floor. Gamma Flip is where the market switches from calm (above) to fast (below).

Use these as zones to plan entries, targets and stops, not as signals on their own.
