FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Still stuck? Ask in the Discord.

Is Bear Squad Trading free?

Yes. Every tool on this site is free to use: Gamma Levels, The Gym, the Data Center, the Economic Calendar, the Journal and the Calculator. A free account saves your journal and lets you post Gym scores. The Discord is free too. The only paid part is Main Cabin, which is optional real-time trade alerts.

Where do the gamma levels come from?

From delayed (about 15 minutes) CBOE options data on QQQ and SPX. We calculate where dealer gamma is stacked, then convert those strikes to NQ and ES prices. Levels refresh every few minutes during market hours, and a snapshot is saved every 15 minutes so you can replay how the walls moved.

What is The Gym?

A practice simulator. It replays a real NQ morning from the past, one 5-minute candle at a time, on a TradingView-style chart. You trade it with a $50K account and a $1,000 daily loss limit. The Daily Challenge gives everyone the same hidden session once a day with a leaderboard, Practice mode is unlimited, and the Sim Challenge is a multi-day evaluation you can pass or fail. It all runs on paper money with real rules.

Do I have to use your strategy?

No. Nothing on the site is built around one strategy. Indicators in the Gym are off by default and you add your own: any EMA length, VWAP or key levels. Trade it however you trade.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser on phone or desktop.

What markets do you cover?

The focus is Nasdaq (NQ, MNQ) and S&P 500 (ES, MES) futures, plus the US economic releases that move them.

Is this financial advice?

No. Everything here is for education and practice. Trading futures and options involves substantial risk of loss.
