Rules of the Gym

The account: $50,000 practice account trading MNQ (Micro Nasdaq, $2 per point per contract). The session runs from the 6:30am open to 8:30am PT on a real past morning. The date stays hidden until you finish.

Daily loss limit: $1,000. Hit it and the account is done for the day, just like a funded account. You can keep training in Practice mode.

Every trade has a stop. You can't risk more than what's left of your daily limit on one trade. Stops and targets fill at their price; if a candle hits both, the stop counts first.

Daily Challenge: one attempt per day, same session for everyone, resets at midnight PT. Log in to post your score to the leaderboard. Practice: unlimited random sessions, not ranked.

Educational simulation only. Not financial advice. Replays use historical NQ futures data; results don't reflect real trading, fills or fees.
